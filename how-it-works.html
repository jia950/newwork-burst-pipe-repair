How It Works: From Your Call to a Repaired Pipe

Three simple steps to reach an independent local plumbing professional for burst pipe repair, frozen pipes and leaks in Buffalo Grove, IL.

When a pipe fails, you want clear steps, not a long process. Call, describe the problem and get connected with a local plumbing professional. This page explains each step so you know what to expect before you call for burst pipe repair in Buffalo Grove, IL or any other pipe problem.

Step 1: Call the Pipe Help Line

Call the number on this page and tell us what is happening. Describe where the water is, whether it is still flowing and what you have already done. If you can safely reach it, close the main shutoff valve first. Not sure where it is? Read how to find your main water shutoff valve. Tell us your area, such as Buffalo Grove, Wheeling or Arlington Heights, so we can point you to the right provider.

Step 2: Get Connected With a Local Provider

You will be connected with a local plumbing professional who serves your area. They confirm availability, arrival time and pricing directly with you. For urgent jobs such as a split pipe or a major leak, see emergency plumbing help. For winter problems, our frozen pipe repair page explains what to expect.

Step 3: Compare and Confirm Before Work Starts

You are in control. Ask about credentials, a written estimate, what is included and what is not, and how long the work will take. In Illinois, plumbers are licensed through the Illinois Department of Public Health, so ask for the license number. Our guide to questions to ask a plumber gives you a checklist. Do not authorize work until you are comfortable with the answers.

What to Have Ready Before You Call

  • Your street or neighborhood and the nearest cross street
  • Where the water is coming from and whether it has been stopped
  • The type of pipe, if you know it (copper, PEX, galvanized or plastic)
  • Photos of the leak or damage, if it is safe to take them
  • Any earlier repairs in the same area

What to Ask the Provider

Ask how the problem will be diagnosed, whether the repair is temporary or permanent and whether nearby pipes should be checked. If the problem may be bigger than one section of pipe, ask about pipe repair and replacement. If water has spread, ask about drying and read our water damage cleanup guidance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to fill anything in online?

No. Just call the number shown on any page and describe the problem.

What if I do not know where my main shutoff valve is?

Shut off water at the nearest fixture if you can, and call right away. Our guide on finding your main water shutoff valve can help for next time.

What should I have ready before I call?

Your location, where the water is coming from and whether it has stopped. Photos of the leak help too, if it is safe to take them.
